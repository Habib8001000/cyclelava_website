Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is Cyclelava / The Cycling Point located?

We are at The Cycling Point in F-8 Markaz, Islamabad. Message us on WhatsApp at 0302 8001000 and we will share the exact location pin.

Can I rent a cycle in Islamabad from you?

Yes. Rental cycles are available from The Cycling Point in F-8 Markaz. Send us the cycle type and duration on WhatsApp and we will confirm the rate and availability.

Do you sell new and used cycles?

Yes. We sell both new and pre-owned bicycles, along with helmets, lights and other cycling accessories, and our workshop services what we sell.

When are the Hikencyc group rides?

Hikencyc rides run every Friday night and every Sunday morning in Islamabad. Message us on WhatsApp for the latest meeting point and time.
