FAQ

Renting, explained.

How much does it cost to rent a cycle in Islamabad?

Rates depend on the cycle type and how long you need it. Send us your cycle type and duration through the form and we will reply on WhatsApp with the price and availability.

Where do I pick up the rental cycle?

From The Cycling Point in F-8 Markaz, Islamabad.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend it, especially before Friday night and Sunday morning Hikencyc rides, because rental cycles are limited.

What do I need to bring to rent?

A valid ID and a refundable deposit may be required. Confirm the details with us when you book.

Can I rent for the Hikencyc group ride?

Yes. Rental cycles are a great way to try a Hikencyc ride. Tell us which ride you are joining when you book.
